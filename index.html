How to read BLOCKWATCH

Live console for the games on Roblox's charts, refreshed about every 10 minutes. Click any game for its dossier and player-count chart, or click a category to narrow everything down.

  • Playing: people in the game right now (concurrent users, or CCU). The little line next to it is the last 24 hours.
  • 1H / 24H / 7D: change in players compared with 1 hour, 1 day and 1 week ago. Comparing with the same time yesterday removes the day/night swing.
  • Likes: thumbs-up share of all votes. 95%+ is excellent; under 80% is weak.
  • Momentum: Rising if up 15%+ over 24h, Falling if down 15%+.
  • Breakout index: players now per 1M lifetime visits. High = new game taking off.
  • Peak time: the hour the game is usually busiest, shown in your local time.
  • Categories: BLOCKWATCH tags like "Incremental / +1 Sim" or "Hangout & Social". Roblox genre is Roblox's own label.
  • Signal feed: automatically detected surges, drops, updates, new chart entries, milestones and record highs.

Data: Roblox's public web APIs, collected by the BLOCKWATCH collector on GitHub. Not affiliated with Roblox.
